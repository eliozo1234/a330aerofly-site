v1.0.02026

Initial release

  • Airbus A330-343 with Rolls-Royce Trent 772B-60 engines, PC (Aerofly FS 4) and mobile (Aerofly FS) editions
  • Fully interactive flight deck: PFD, ND, ECAM with all system pages, two MCDUs, FCU, overhead, pedestal
  • Airbus fly by wire with normal, alternate and direct laws and all protections; autoland
  • A330 speed envelope, masses, centre of gravity and tail strike attitudes
  • Eight wheel brake thermal model per bogie, BRAKES HOT, brake fans
  • ROW/ROP runway overrun warning and protection
  • GPWS, radio altitude, RETARD, V1 and PITCH PITCH callouts
  • Trent 700 3D sound set
  • Fourteen airline liveries
