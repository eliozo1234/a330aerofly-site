The aircraft

Airbus A330-300

The A330-300 is Airbus' long range twin engine wide body, in service since 1994 and still in production in its A330-900neo form. With a 60.3 m wing, a 63.7 m fuselage and a maximum takeoff weight of 233 tonnes, it carries up to 440 passengers over 11,750 km, the aircraft of choice for medium and long haul routes across Europe, Asia and the Americas.

This edition models the Rolls-Royce Trent 772B-60 powered A330-343: the Airbus fly by wire flight deck shared with the A320 and A340 families, two four wheel main gear bogies, a three spool high bypass engine and the systems that go with a wide body, from a 97 tonne fuel system to eight independently cooled brakes.

Flight deck

A fully interactive Airbus cockpit.

Every switch, knob, lever and push button works, with its own sound. PFD and ND with all display modes, the ECAM engine/warning display and every system page, two MCDUs, FCU, overhead and pedestal, laid out to the real panel dimensions, with adjustable display and panel lighting. Built for mouse, touch and VR.

Systems

The systems of a wide body, all running.

A complete electrical network with batteries, generators, buses and individual loads; three hydraulic circuits; bleed air, air conditioning and pressurisation; a fuel system with variable payload and a correct centre of gravity; eight braked wheels with a thermal model per bogie. The flight warning computer watches all of it and raises the right ECAM caution at the right flight phase.

Engines and sound

Two Trent 700s, heard from every seat.

The Trent 772B-60 is simulated thermodynamically, fan, compressors, burner, turbines and reversers under a FADEC, and scaled to its real 316 kN. A dedicated Trent sound set covers spool up, idle whine, takeoff roar and reverse, played in 3D from the engines, the cabin and the flight deck, with authentic altitude, V1 and PITCH PITCH callouts.

Flight model and exterior

233 tonnes of fly by wire.

A multibody airframe that flexes under load, Airbus normal, alternate and direct laws with all envelope protections, autoland, auto brake and ground spoilers. Outside: a high resolution model with animated doors, gear, bogies, flaps, slats and spoilers, dynamic exterior lights and fourteen airline liveries.

Key features

What is simulated, group by group.

Physics simulation

  • Full multibody aircraft that bends under load
  • Realistic ground physics, gear compression and damping
  • Four wheel bogies, gear drag and tail strike collision
  • Thermodynamic engine simulation with compressor, burner, turbines, fan, reversers and nozzle under a FADEC
  • Cold and dark start up

Aerodynamics and flight controls

  • Native wing flex
  • Independent slats, flaps and individual spoilers
  • Fly by wire with normal, alternate and direct laws, flight, ground and flare modes
  • All flight envelope protections
  • Automatic ground spoilers after touchdown or rejected takeoff
  • A330 speed envelope: VMO 330 kt, MMO 0.86, VFE per configuration, VLE 250 kt

Aircraft systems

  • Electrical network with batteries, buses, generators and individual loads
  • Three hydraulic circuits
  • Bleed air, cross bleed engine start, packs and pressurisation
  • Fuel system with variable payload and centre of gravity
  • Eight braked wheels with a thermal model per bogie, BRAKES HOT logic and brake fans
  • Flight warning computer with hundreds of ECAM cautions and warnings, flight phases, TO CONFIG and TRIM DISAGREE checks

Autopilot and FMS

Questions

Which simulator versions are supported?

The PC edition runs in Aerofly FS 4 (Steam, Windows). The mobile edition runs in Aerofly FS on iOS and Android. Each edition is a separate purchase.

How do I install it?

PC: unzip the download into your Documents\Aerofly FS 4\aircraft folder and start the simulator. Mobile: follow the import steps on the support page, it takes two minutes.

Are updates included?

Yes. Every update for the edition you bought is free, delivered through your Lemon Squeezy download link.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds follow the Lemon Squeezy policy. If something does not work on your setup, write to us first, most issues are solved in one email.

Does it work in VR?

The PC edition works in VR exactly as any Aerofly FS 4 aircraft does.

Is this an official IPACS product?

No. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by IPACS, the developer of Aerofly FS.
