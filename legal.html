Who publishes this site, how purchases work and what data is processed.

Publisher

This website is published by A330 Aerofly Development, an independent developer of add on aircraft for Aerofly FS. Contact: see the support page.

Sales and payment

The A330-300 for Aerofly FS is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Payment, invoicing, taxes and refunds are handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own terms and privacy policy. This website contains no shop, no cart and no account: purchase links lead to Lemon Squeezy.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not track visitors. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The trailer is embedded from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) and loads only after you click play. The waitlist is a Google Form: the data you enter there is processed by Google and used solely to inform you about the release.
