Installation

Step by step guides for PC, Android and iOS are on the Installation page.

Open the installation guide

First flight

Start from the Takeoff position for a ready to fly aircraft, or from Cold and Dark to run the full start up. Enter your weights and cost index on the MCDU INIT B page and your V speeds and flaps on PERF TAKE OFF: the takeoff trim is computed from the centre of gravity you enter. Watch the ECAM: if the configuration is wrong, it will tell you before you line up.

Troubleshooting

  • The aircraft is not in the list (PC): check that the folder is exactly Documents\Aerofly FS 4\aircraft\a330 and contains a330.tmc.
  • A livery is missing: liveries are chosen in the aircraft's livery menu; make sure you installed the latest version of the package.
  • No engine sound after installation: restart the simulator once after installing.
  • Updates: download the latest version from your Gumroad library and replace the a330 folder (PC) or the .tme file (mobile).
