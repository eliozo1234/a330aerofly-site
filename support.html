Support

Installation, first flight and help. Most questions are answered here; for the rest, write to us.

Installation, PC (Aerofly FS 4)

  1. Download the ZIP from your Lemon Squeezy order page.
  2. Unzip it. You get a folder named a330.
  3. Move that folder into Documents\Aerofly FS 4\aircraft\ (create the aircraft folder if it does not exist).
  4. Start Aerofly FS 4. The A330-300 appears in the aircraft list under Airliners.

Installation, mobile (Aerofly FS)

  1. Download the mobile package from your Lemon Squeezy order page to your phone or tablet.
  2. Open Aerofly FS, go to Settings → Aircraft add ons and choose Import.
  3. Select the downloaded file. The aircraft is installed in the app and appears in the aircraft list.
  4. Liveries are selected from the aircraft's livery menu, like any stock aircraft.

First flight

Start from the Takeoff position for a ready to fly aircraft, or from Cold and Dark to run the full start up. Enter your weights and cost index on the MCDU INIT B page and your V speeds and flaps on PERF TAKE OFF: the takeoff trim is computed from the centre of gravity you enter. Watch the ECAM: if the configuration is wrong, it will tell you before you line up.

Troubleshooting

  • The aircraft is not in the list (PC): check that the folder is exactly Documents\Aerofly FS 4\aircraft\a330 and contains a330.tmc.
  • A livery is missing: liveries are chosen in the aircraft's livery menu; make sure you installed the latest version of the package.
  • No engine sound after installation: restart the simulator once after installing.
  • Updates: download the latest version from the same Lemon Squeezy link and replace the a330 folder.
